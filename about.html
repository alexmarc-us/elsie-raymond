About

Elsie Raymond

  • PNWA 2024 · 3rd Place
  • Viable Paradise alum
  • SFWA member
  • HWA member

Elsie Raymond is a mom and writer from the Pacific Northwest.

Elsie writes speculative fiction, horror and raves about food and books on her Substack. She is an alumnus of the Viable Paradise workshop and a member of the Science Fiction Writers of America and Horror Writers of America. Her work has been published in literary magazines, anthologies, and a podcast. Her dystopian romance manuscript won third place in the 2024 Pacific Northwest Writers Association unpublished novel contest in Speculative Fiction.
