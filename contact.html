Full

Elsie Raymond is a dark speculative fiction writer, influenced by her abiding love for pop culture and genre fiction. Her writing slips between genres and pairs rich worldbuilding with unhinged ideas.

Raymond was longlisted for Uncharted’s Horror Short Story contest, won third place in the PNWA unpublished novel contest and won the Nancy Freedman Award for Creative Writing.

A member of the BARN Writers’ Studio steering committee, Raymond helps organize classes and events to build the writing community within greater Kitsap County. She is a co-founder of the annual Story Island Writers Conference for genre fiction writers on Bainbridge Island. She is a proud alumnus of the Viable Paradise writing workshop.

Raymond lives in the Pacific Northwest and spends most of her time in a book nook, reading about the cosmos, falling in love with broody romance leads, and scaring herself witless with haunted house stories.
